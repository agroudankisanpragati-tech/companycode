# Crop planning depends on local context

There is no single crop choice that can be recommended from a crop name or a broad state-level label alone. ICAR's resource-based crop-planning discussion says planning should align with agroecological conditions, available soil and water resources, and local requirements.

## Information to gather before discussing options

1. The farm's district or agroecological area and the intended season.
2. The field's soil-test information, if available, and known drainage or salinity concerns.
3. Water source and likely availability during the crop cycle.
4. The farmer's intended crop, variety (if known), planting window, and production goals.
5. Relevant local crop advisories from the State Agricultural University, KVK, or agriculture department.

These details help determine which locally validated source is relevant. This article does **not** rank crops, recommend a variety, predict profit, or replace a district-specific package of practices. If the needed local source is not available, respond `UNKNOWN / REQUIRES SOURCE` and ask a local extension expert.
