# Water planning must match the crop and field

Irrigation guidance should not be reduced to one schedule for every farm. TNAU's paddy guidance separates rice systems and variety durations and explains that water needs depend on agroclimatic conditions and soil characteristics. That is a paddy example, not a schedule for other crops or locations.

## Before giving crop-specific irrigation guidance

1. Confirm the crop, variety if known, planting method, and growth stage.
2. Ask about the field's soil and drainage, water source, and recent rainfall or irrigation.
3. Retrieve a current local advisory for that crop and production system from the relevant State Agricultural University, KVK, or agriculture department.
4. Explain only the schedule or decision rule supported by that source, including its region and conditions.
5. If no suitable local source is available, say `UNKNOWN / REQUIRES SOURCE`; do not invent an interval, water depth, or quantity.

This article contains no watering interval, depth, or volume. A farmer's current rainfall, soil moisture, and water availability are live/local conditions and should not be guessed from a static article.
