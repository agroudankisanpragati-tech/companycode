## Source-selection notes

- Use the direct technical publication/page, not a search snippet, as the actual evidence during review.
- A source can justify only the claims within its stated crop, region, and conditions.
- Current chemical use instructions must come from a current official crop/target recommendation and the product label. No product-specific advice is included in this starter set.
- Administrative scheme material is excluded even when encountered on an otherwise useful government portal.
